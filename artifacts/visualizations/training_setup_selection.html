Animal Agent — Training setup selection

Retained setup: randomized scripted interactions

The randomized scripted setup has higher reward per decision for both agents in training and validation, and higher state-action coverage ratios for both agents. Synthetic-user runs have higher absolute unique-state and unique-pair counts, but lower coverage ratios and lower rewards.
